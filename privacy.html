Privacy Policy

Home Family Assistant
Last updated: September 28, 2026

Home Family Assistant is a private household messaging service used by members of a single household to manage household tasks, shopping lists, calendars, reminders, and related information.

Information We Process

The service may process mobile phone numbers, SMS/MMS messages, message metadata, and information voluntarily provided by participating household members. This information is used solely to provide and operate the household assistant service.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to process household requests, update shared calendars and lists, provide reminders, maintain limited household preferences, troubleshoot the service, and operate related automation features.

Service Providers

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the service, such as telecommunications, cloud hosting, automation, calendar, task-management, and artificial-intelligence providers, solely as necessary to provide the service.

No Marketing or Sale of Mobile Information

Mobile phone numbers, messaging opt-in information, and messaging consent are not sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Messaging Terms

Message frequency varies based on household use. Message and data rates may apply. Users may reply STOP to stop receiving messages and HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

Information may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain household records or preferences, troubleshoot issues, or comply with applicable legal requirements.
